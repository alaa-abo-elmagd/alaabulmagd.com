ABOUT

Crafting The Extraordinary

I'm Alaa Abulmagd — a creative designer and photographer born and raised in Alexandria, Egypt. My journey started in a small bedroom studio with a borrowed camera and a burning curiosity: what makes a brand impossible to forget? That question has driven every project since.

Over 5+ years I've built visual systems for restaurants, clinics, tech startups, and lifestyle brands — always with the same obsessive attention to detail. Clients like Cortigiano, ME Career, and Kotobna trusted me to give their brands a soul: cohesive identities that work at every touchpoint, from Instagram stories to printed menus to cinematic campaign reels.

My philosophy is simple — strategy before aesthetics, story before style. Beautiful work that doesn't convert is just decoration. I design with purpose, shoot with intention, and edit to emotion.
